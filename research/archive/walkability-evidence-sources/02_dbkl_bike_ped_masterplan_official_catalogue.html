Background of the Department

The Department of Infrastructure Planning was established on July 1, 2011, with the purpose of planning and overseeing the development of infrastructure and public facilities in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.

Vision

To establish efficient planning and control of infrastructure development in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.

Mision

1. Controlling infrastructure development work through the approval of Earthworks, Roads and Drainage, Entrance Culverts, and Internal Circulation Flow and Parking Space Certification.2. Planning road and drainage infrastructure development by processing, reviewing, and providing comments related to the submission of road reserve land at the planning approval stage.3. Ensuring that road and drainage infrastructure built by developers comply with approved plans and function properly.

The main functions of this Department are:

  1. Planning the road submission requirements and upgrading existing roads based on Traffic Impact Studies for development projects in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.

  2. Planning the submission requirements for main drains and retention ponds for development projects in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.

  3. Controlling development in terms of land work plan approval, roads and drainage, parking spaces, stormwater management system (MSMA), and issuing support G17 (Roads and Drainage) for CCC issuance.

  4. Processing permit applications for the transport of materials.

  5. Playing a role in assigning addresses to each building, declaring private roads as public roads, preparing engineering road plans, keeping records of standard plans, and acting as the secretariat for the Geographical Name Technical Committee for Street Names in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.
