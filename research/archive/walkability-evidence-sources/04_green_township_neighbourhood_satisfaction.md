# Source record: Neighbourhood satisfaction in a Malaysian green township

- **Citation:** Tan, Teck Hong (2016), “Neighbourhood satisfaction: responses from residents of green townships in Malaysia,” *International Journal of Housing Markets and Analysis*, 9(1), 137–155.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1108/IJHMA-01-2015-0001

## Study and findings

The study distributed 300 self-administered questionnaires to residents of a green-accredited Malaysian township and analysed 190 returned surveys. Integrated connectivity/accessibility and environmental quality significantly influenced neighbourhood satisfaction. Respondents were comparatively dissatisfied with security and community participation.

## Why it is in this pack

It supports the claim that township quality is not just about adding amenities: safe access, connectivity, environmental quality, security, and community participation shape residents’ satisfaction.

## Evidence caveat

This is a single-township, cross-sectional study. It should not be treated as nationally representative or as proof that any one intervention causes higher satisfaction.
