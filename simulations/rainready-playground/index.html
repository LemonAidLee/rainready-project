Assumptions in this playground
Map64 × 40 cells, each 50 m × 50 m (0.25 ha). Terrain is invented: high upstream, a meandering river, a flat low-lying zone downstream.
Runoff shareRoofs and roads 85% of rain, green land 30%, wetland park 25%, river and ponds 100%. Illustrative values.
Water movementEach simulated minute, water moves towards the lowest neighbouring cell. The outlet at the right edge carries a set amount per second. Drain D-07 cannot empty while the river is higher than the low-lying zone.
Risk bandsBands come from how much water sits on a cell in this model. In the real tool, terrain error is about 1 m in built-up areas, so it shows bands and never a depth.
Storage needVolume balance for the whole storm: rain × runoff share × area, minus outlet capacity × duration. The real tool uses MSMA 2 design rainfall.
OwnersInvented for teaching. The real tool records sources and confirms owners with councils and the drainage department.
