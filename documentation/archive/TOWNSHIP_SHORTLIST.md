# Township Shortlist for the Teduh Study

> **Superseded (10 Oct 2026).** This compared sites for the shade idea. The chosen site is Bandar Bukit Raja; see [TOWNSHIP_GAP_ANALYSIS.md](../supporting/TOWNSHIP_GAP_ANALYSIS.md).

Purpose: choose **one SDP township** for the detailed 3D heat model, and optionally a second for a light screening. All facts below come from press, property listings and developer brochures found online. **Nothing here has been checked on the ground**, and several figures conflict between sources. Verify each point on OpenStreetMap, satellite imagery and Street View before relying on it.

Legend: **✓** looks favourable, **~** mixed or unknown, **✗** unfavourable, **?** nothing found.

---

## 1. Selection criteria

| # | Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owned/managed by SDP | Strategic fit; SDP can only pilot on land it controls |
| 2 | Good public data (OSM buildings, clear imagery) | The model and scoring run on public data |
| 3 | Real walking destinations and a transit stop | Teduh ranks routes by people served |
| 4 | Mixed street orientations | Shows the difference between hot and cooler streets |
| 5 | Residents reachable for the survey | Evidence for the pain |
| 6 | A story for the what-if mode | Planners can test options before building |

## 3. Recommendation

**Primary: Putra Heights station catchment (about 500 m around the LRT station).**
- A compact area suits the 3D model.
- A real transit stop, destinations and a covered walkway mean you can test whether the walk is actually covered and where gaps remain.
- Risk: the covered walkway may already solve the problem on that route. If so, look at the other approaches to the station.

**Alternate: one precinct of City of Elmina** (for example around the mall or Central Park).
- Best story for the what-if mode, because the township is still being built.
- Risk: it is large and promoted as walkable. Pick one precinct, and focus on shade on existing paths.

**Reference only: Bukit Jelutong** as an example of mature tree-lined streets, to show what good canopy looks like.

**Skip for the main study: Bandar Bukit Raja.** There is no rail within walking distance, so the walk-to-transit case is weak. It stays relevant as a flood-idea context.

## 4. Not covered

I found nothing usable on **USJ Heights, Denai Alam, Ara Damansara, Serenia City, Ainsdale or Melawati** (only that Melawati is about 900 acres and under 15 km from KLCC). SDP is also buying about 557 acres in Kulai, Johor, but the first phase isn't due until 2028, so it isn't a candidate. Search these separately if you want more options.

---

## 5. Quick checks before committing (about an hour each)

1. Open each candidate on OpenStreetMap: are buildings, footpaths and bus or rail stops mapped?
2. Look at satellite and Street View imagery: is there visible shade along the main routes, and what is the street layout?
3. Check the SDP website and press for the current list and phase status of each township.
4. Search Facebook for resident groups for each township, and note their size and rules.
5. Ask SDP through the official Telegram channel whether a township is preferred or off limits.
