# SDP Township Gap Analysis

**Question:** across SDP's townships, which one feature or service are residents lacking that would benefit people or the planet?
**Method:** public sources only (press, SDP releases, resident memoranda). **No site visits.** Absence of a complaint in the press does not mean absence of a problem, and many townships returned nothing. Treat this as a first screening, not a finding.

Confidence: **H** high, **M** medium, **L** low.

---

## 1. Headline

**The best-documented gap is drainage and flood coordination at Bandar Bukit Raja (BBR), Klang.** It is township-specific, repeated, organised by residents, and acknowledged by SDP's own management. It is stronger than anything found for shade/heat in any SDP township.

## 3. Patterns across townships

| Pattern | Where seen | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Drainage / flood, with split responsibility** | BBR (strong); Putra Heights after the fire; Bukit Jelutong interchange | Water crosses council and developer boundaries, so no one sees the whole system |
| **"Who is responsible?" blame-shifting** | BBR: councils and developers shift blame; Melawati 2017: developer pointed to council | A coordination and information gap, not only a construction gap |
| **Last-mile and rail transit** | Elmina, BBR (KTM about 8 km away), Ainsdale | Backed nationally by Ipsos and the Transport Minister's comments on landed estates |
| **Hazard communication** | Putra Heights | Real, but tragic and sensitive |
| **Shade / heat** | None found in any township | No documented complaint; the Teduh case rests on general studies |

## 4. What this does to the project choice

The BBR record answers the main objections to the flood idea:

| Earlier objection to flood | What the BBR record shows |
|---|---|
| "No township-level evidence" | Documented repeated flooding, a 583-resident memorandum, and SDP's own statements |
| "SDP can't act, drains belong to councils" | Residents ask SDP directly for more ponds in Phase 1; SDP says it has gone beyond its obligations within its developments and uses wetlands and its town park for retention ([SDP](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sustainability/urbanbiodiversity/spotlight-bandar-bukit-raja.html)) |
| "No data for the demo" | Public layers (terrain, rainfall, rivers) plus the documented hotspots (Jalan Makyong, Jalan Sumazau) let you build a real demo of one catchment |

**Revised judgement (still my own, wide margin):** flood at BBR now looks stronger than Teduh. Teduh already carries the umbrella objection and has no township-specific complaint. Flood at BBR has documented resident pain, an SDP-acknowledged priority and a clear "no one sees the whole catchment" gap.

### A possible framing (not an implementation plan)

> **Residents and managers in flood-prone townships have no shared, up-to-date view of how water moves across council and developer boundaries, so retention capacity and drain upgrades are argued over after each flood instead of planned before it.**

Candidate features: a catchment view linking upstream development to downstream flooding; a retention-pond capacity and "what-if" check for new ponds; a responsibility map showing which agency owns each drain, pond and river reach; and pre-storm alerts for specific streets.

**Team fit:** the mechanical engineer covers hydraulics; the architecture student covers retention ponds and wetland design (blue-green infrastructure); the three EE students cover sensors, data pipeline, alerts and dashboard.

## 5. Cautions

- **Sensitivity.** The project describes a problem SDP is publicly dealing with. Frame it as helping SDP's stated priority, not criticising it. SDP gave no public reply to the May 2024 memorandum in the articles I read.
- **Contested causes.** Residents blame upstream development and unimproved rivers (Sungai Puloh, Kapar Kecil); SDP cites inadequate natural drainage. Don't pick sides in the deck.
- **National flood losses fell in 2025** and are dominated by east-coast river floods. Use BBR evidence, not national totals, to carry the story.
- **No on-site validation.** Everything here is from media and a resident petition, and the flood depth figures are residents' accounts.
- **Don't build on the Putra Heights tragedy** without a very good reason and sensitive handling.
