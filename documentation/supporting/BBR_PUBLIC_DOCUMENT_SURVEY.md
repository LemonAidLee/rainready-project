# Bandar Bukit Raja: Public Document Survey

**Purpose:** find public documents with pond, drainage and flood-project details for Bandar Bukit Raja (BBR) and its upstream neighbour Setia Alam, so the model does not rest only on assumptions. Companion to [FLOOD_FEASIBILITY_SURVEY.md](FLOOD_FEASIBILITY_SURVEY.md) and [FLOOD_PITCH_DECK.md](FLOOD_PITCH_DECK.md).
**Searched:** 10 October 2026, online only. Confidence: **H** primary document or two outlets, **M** one reliable outlet, **L** unverified or search summary only.

---

## 1. Headline

**Some real numbers exist, and they change two things in the pitch.** SDP's own BBR booklet gives pond and storage figures, and several government programmes are already under way on both sides of the council boundary. The documents also show a problem that fits our idea closely: **many separate projects and unmanned ponds, with no single shared picture.**

## 4. What this changes

### 4.1 Corrections to the pitch deck

1. **"Four shallow ponds" is the residents' 2024 account.** Do not present it as current fact. Say "residents said in 2024" and acknowledge the Townpark storage and the Taman Mutiara pond.
2. **Do not say nothing is being done.** Several projects exist upstream and downstream. The stronger claim is that they are **separate programmes under separate councils with no shared view.**
3. **Do not use the KLASS figure** or the RM574.5m figure until verified.

### 4.2 Strengthened claims

| New fact | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **300+ unmanned ponds, about 55 taken over by DID** | Direct evidence of an ownership gap, which supports the responsibility map. Cite Bernama, May 2025 |
| **RM150m upstream (MBSA) while downstream works sit under MBDK** | A concrete example of two programmes on one river system |
| **SDP's own 50,000 m³ maximum storage figure** | Lets the pond what-if start from a real number for one facility |
| **Taman Mutiara pond delayed by pump-capacity issues** | Shows why a pre-construction check of discharge and storage is useful. Frame neutrally |

### 4.3 Assumption ledger updates

| Item | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Townpark storage | Unknown | 16,000 / 34,000 / 50,000 m³ (normal / additional / maximum), SDP booklet |
| Taman Mutiara pond area | Unknown | About 2 ha; depth still assumed |
| Total pond area | Unknown | 130 acres, SDP booklet (scope and definition to confirm) |
| Other ponds, drains, pumps | Unknown | Still unknown |

**Still assumed:** the depth of the Taman Mutiara pond, other pond volumes, drain capacities, and pump discharge rates.
